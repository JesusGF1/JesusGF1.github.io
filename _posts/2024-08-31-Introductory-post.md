There are a ton of things I want to learn, but life always gets in the way. To hold myself accountable, I want to build and learn in public.

This blog is where I will keep those notes: short write-ups on the papers I read, the tools I build, and the things I break along the way, mostly around neuroscience, genomics, deep learning, and a bit of web development. Some posts will be polished, others will be rough working notes. The point is to keep a record and to think out loud.
